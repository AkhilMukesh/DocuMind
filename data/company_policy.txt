Company Policy

Employees are expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues and customers respectfully, and protect confidential company information.
Employees can work 3 days from home and 2 days from office after manager apporvals.
Work hours and attendance
Employees should follow the working hours agreed with their manager and notify their manager as soon as possible if they will be absent or late.

Security and privacy
Use strong passwords, keep account credentials private, and access company information only when needed for your work. Report suspected security incidents promptly.

Use of company equipment
Company devices and accounts should be used responsibly and kept secure. Employees should follow applicable software licensing and data-handling requirements.

Time off
Submit leave requests to your manager in advance whenever practical. Leave is subject to the applicable company process and local requirements.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or the People team.
